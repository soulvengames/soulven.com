Community Rules & Privacy Policy

📜 Server Rules

😃 1. Be cool, kind, and respectful to one another.

📇 2. Keep your Discord profile appropriate.

✉️ 3. Do not spam.

🔔 4. Do not @mention or direct message the staff. See Mods below in the channel for exceptions.

📣 5. No self-promotion or advertisements.

🛡️ 6. No personal information.

🤬 7. No hate speech or harmful language.

🏛️ 8. No political or religious topics.

🚨 9. No piracy, sexual, NSFW, or otherwise suspicious content.

🤔 10. Rules are subject to common sense.




📜 MODS

Our moderation team is here to make sure this cozy little corner of Discord stays warm and safe for everyone. 🍵 If you ever come across any problems related to our server, don't hesitate to tag them in the channel. ❤️ Quick heads-up: The moderation team won't handle issues through DMs between members. If you run into any trouble there, it's best to report it directly to Discord's Trust & Safety and block the user.


📜 Privacy Policy for Discord

This Discord server is operated by Soulven Games, an independent game development studio based in Germany. We use this server as an official communication platform for our community, players, and partners.

Data Controller

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the data controller for personal data processed in connection with this server.

What Data Is Collected

When participating in our Discord server, the following types of data may be processed:

Your Discord username, user ID, and profile picture Messages and content you share on the server Roles, join date, and interactions Activity statistics (e.g., participation, XP level) Responses to moderation commands, forms, or custom commands

Bot Usage Notice:

We use Mee6 Bot to manage community features such as leveling, moderation, welcome messages, and event organization. Mee6 may process data related to your: Username and Discord ID Activity on the server (e.g., messages sent, time online) Participation in automated features (e.g., XP system, ranks, polls)

Please refer to the Mee6 privacy policy for details on their own data practices: 👉 https://mee6.xyz/terms.pdf

Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed exclusively for: Community management and moderation Enhancing server engagement (e.g., XP system) Organizing events, rewards, or community milestones Preventing misuse and maintaining a safe environment

Legal Basis

Processing is carried out based on: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – to fulfill community participation as a user-initiated service Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in providing a functional and safe Discord server

Data Retention

We retain user data as long as necessary for the above purposes. You can request deletion of your personal data or posted content at any time via the contact email listed below.

Your Rights (under GDPR)

You have the right to: Request access to your data, Request correction or deletion, Object to processing, Request data portability or Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority
