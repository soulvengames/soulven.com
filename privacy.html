📜 Privacy Policy (Soulven UG)



1. Introduction

Soulven UG ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website [insert website URL], engage with our community, or access our games and related services.

This Policy is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable German data protection law.



2. Data We Collect

When you interact with our website or services, we may collect the following types of data:

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and referrer URLs.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, links clicked (including external links such as Steam, Discord, social media), and session duration.
  • Marketing & analytics data: Information provided by tracking technologies (see Section 4).
  • Voluntary data: Information you choose to share with us, e.g., when joining our Discord, signing up for a newsletter, or contacting us via email.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16.



3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To operate and improve our website and services.
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior (e.g., where users come from and which pages are most visited).
  • To measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
  • To enable community features (Discord, social media integrations).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.



4. Tracking & Analytics Technologies

Our website uses cookies, tracking pixels, and UTM parameters to measure traffic and improve marketing performance.

The following tools are in use or may be used in the future:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) – to analyze website traffic.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) – to measure ad performance on Meta platforms.
  • TikTok Pixel (TikTok Technology Ltd.) – to track ad conversions from TikTok.
  • Reddit Pixel (Reddit Inc.) – to measure performance of Reddit Ads campaigns.
  • Steam Referrer Tracking (Valve Corporation) – when a user visits our Steam Store page, UTM parameters are passed to Steam. Valve may record referral data, but we do not receive personally identifiable information from Valve.

5. Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager (“GTM”), a service provided by Google Ireland Limited. GTM itself does not collect or store personal data. The service enables us to manage and integrate tracking and marketing tags on our website in a centralized manner. GTM may trigger other tags, which may in turn collect data. We ensure that GTM is used in compliance with the applicable data protection regulations and that no personal data is transmitted directly through GTM itself. For more information, please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/.



6. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): when you accept cookies or tracking.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): improving our services, fraud prevention, and security.
  • Contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing services you request.


7. Data Sharing

We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., analytics providers, hosting services). All such providers process data on our behalf and are bound by strict contractual obligations under GDPR.

We do not share personal data with third parties for unrelated marketing purposes.



8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law. Tracking and analytics data are typically stored between 14 and 26 months (depending on the provider’s settings).



9. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a data subject, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”, Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
