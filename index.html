ABOUT US

Founded in 2016 as blackHound Studios and reimagined in 2023 as Soulven Games, we are an indie game studio based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, with a vision to redefine the landscape of 3D and game development.

At Soulven Games, our mission is to become not just a leader but the best employer in the realm of 3D game development. We firmly believe that by fostering an outstanding work environment, we can create exceptional products and deliver unparalleled service. Our approach is holistic; while we value the economic aspects of our business, we seamlessly integrate them into our broader processes, ensuring a balance between profitability and creativity.

The essence of our name, Soulven, intertwines two powerful concepts: 'Soul' and 'Ven', derived from 'invention'. This fusion represents our commitment to nurturing interpersonal relationships while continuously driving innovation. We strive to create a workplace where creativity thrives, and our team feels valued and inspired.

Our dedicated team of developers, designers, and artists work collaboratively to push the boundaries of what is possible in game development. We focus on creating immersive, engaging, and innovative gaming experiences that captivate players around the world.

Join us at Soulven Games and be part of a team that values creativity, innovation, and a strong sense of community. Together, we are shaping the future of 3D game development.
