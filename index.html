Our current focus is My Dear Emma, a calm narrative adventure about grief, family memory and self-discovery. The project represents the direction we want Soulven to stand for: emotionally grounded stories, atmospheric worlds and modern real-time technology.

  My Dear Emma

Narrative Adventure | Exploration | Puzzle | PC |

In Development

My Dear Emma is a relaxed first-person narrative adventure about Emma Wiedman, a biologist and nature photographer who returns to her grandparents’ estate after the death of her grandfather. Guided by a notebook, a map and a final scavenger hunt, players uncover childhood memories, family secrets and the question of what Emma truly wants her life to become.

ABOUT US

About Soulven

An independent studio for meaningful interactive products.

Soulven is based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. We are a focused team of developers, artists and technical designers working across games, real-time 3D and software development.

Our own games shape the standards we bring into every project: clear direction, strong atmosphere, responsible technology choices and a high level of craft. Alongside our original IPs, we selectively support organizations that need reliable full-stack development, applied 3D systems, simulations, training environments or technical project leadership.

The name Soulven reflects our belief that the strongest products are built where human meaning meets invention. We value small senior teams, honest collaboration and long-term thinking over short-term production volume.

Soul-DrivenWe build around emotion, purpose and the people who will experience the final product.
Invention-LedWe use modern technology deliberately, with a focus on maintainability, scalability and impact.
Game-MindedWe bring atmosphere, interactivity and player-focused thinking into our games and client work.
Built to LastWe prefer clear architecture, sustainable decisions and partnerships with real long-term value.
