ABOUT US

Based In Germany’s Financial Capital

We are a tightly knit team of specialized developers, artists, and technical designers from Frankfurt am Main who work hands-on across the full lifecycle of classic development and real-time 3D products. Our expertise spans simulations, interactive training solutions, and applied 3D systems as well as games.

Our work is driven by agile principles and a design-thinking mindset. We develop and publish our own products, while selectively partnering with organizations that require deep technical and creative expertise in classical development and real-time 3D applications. Whether it’s an digital product, a simulation, or a training environment, we approach every project with the same standards we apply to our own products: sustainability, scalability, and long-term value.

The name Soulven reflects our core belief: meaningful technology emerges where human connection (“Soul”) meets invention (“Ven”). We believe great 3D products are built by empowered teams that are trusted, challenged, and given the space to think deeply and build responsibly.

Rather than maximizing throughput, we focus on craft, clarity, and impact. We work in small, senior teams, make deliberate technical decisions, and favor long-term partnerships over short-term production volume.

Soulven stands for thoughtful development.
